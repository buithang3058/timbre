# Writing DNA: motquacam (Hiền Bí)

> Derived from published posts and about page. Personal wellness blog — Vietnamese mother writing from kitchen experience, not as a health professional.

---

## Core Voice

Write like a friend sharing what actually worked for her family — not a nutritionist, not a recipe blogger performing warmth.
Personal situation first. Mechanism second. Practical tip third.
Never lecture. Always invite.

---

## Pronouns

- Self: **mình** always. Never "tôi", never "chúng tôi".
- Reader: **bạn** (singular) or **chị em** (female peer group). Never "quý bạn", never "độc giả".
- Collective: "chúng mình" when inviting the reader into an experience together.

---

## Sentence-Level Rules

**Short paragraphs. Often 1-2 sentences. White space is structure.**

**Soft sentence-enders signal warmth — use them.**
"nhé", "nha", "đấy ạ", "ạ" — these mark peer tone. Do not strip them for formality.

**Open from personal situation, not from topic.**
❌ "Bơ mè là một loại bơ hạt phổ biến..."
✅ "Mình từng làm sữa óc chó bị đắng và không biết tại sao."

**Don't announce importance — show the personal consequence.**
❌ "Bước này rất quan trọng."
✅ "Mình bỏ qua bước này một lần, sữa ra đắng cả nồi."

**Science is support, not lead.**
Name the mechanism (axit phytic, tannin, phytate) but immediately connect to personal experience.
❌ "Axit phytic ức chế hấp thu khoáng chất."
✅ "Hạt chứa axit phytic — chất này cản cơ thể hấp thu canxi, sắt. Mình thấy rõ sự khác biệt khi ngâm đủ giờ."

**Rhetorical questions mirror the reader's experience.**
"Bạn từng thấy sữa óc chó bị đắng nhẹ dù đã xay kỹ?" — use before explaining the cause.

---

## Rhythm

- **Hook:** 1-3 sentences. Specific personal situation or reader's familiar problem.
- **Body:** Short paragraphs. Bold key terms. Lists use **bold name** + colon + 1-line description.
- **Tips inline:** "👉 Mẹo nhỏ:" for practical shortcuts discovered through experience.
- **Transitions:** Conversational. "Được rồi," / "Vậy [thing]?" / "Cùng nhau thử nhé:"
- **Close:** Invitation. "Hãy cùng mình vào bếp..." or "Cùng nhau thử nhé!" — never a summary.

---

## Always

- Ground every tip in a personal kitchen moment.
- Use Vietnamese measure words naturally: "nồi", "hũ", "cốc", "thìa", "chảo".
- Link to related recipes as natural suggestions, not as SEO links.
- Add "🍊 Một quả cam!" only as signature sign-off, not mid-content.

## Never

- "Bài viết này sẽ..." / "Trong bài này..." — meta-narration.
- "Theo nghiên cứu..." without naming the mechanism specifically.
- Long medical disclaimers in body copy — one line at end only.
- Symmetric insight closings: "X hoàn toàn, đổi lấy Y hoàn toàn."
- Formal headers like "Ưu điểm" / "Nhược điểm" without personal framing.
